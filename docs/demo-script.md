# Demo Script — My Agent ʻOhana (ETHOnline 2026)

**For:** Shaka's real voice over the screen recording (no AI voiceover — banned by the rules and by our own canon).
**Length:** ~3 minutes (comfort zone 2:40–3:20; a tight 2-minute cut is mapped at the bottom).
**Flow:** the six steps of `specs/whitepaper.html` §2, in order. **TIMECODES ARE LOCKS** — the screen recording is cut to hit these marks; read at your natural pace and each block lands on its beat.
**How to record:** phone or mic, quiet room, one breath between blocks. Don't perform it — talk it like you're showing Kevin the thing at the camp. If a line feels written, say it your way; the timestamps survive paraphrase.

## 2:00–2:35 · STEP 5 — The one-word revoke 🌋 *(the peak — slow down)*

**Screen:** on Terri's card → *One-word revoke* → modal: *"One word: withdraw — TERRI"* → the word `withdraw` typed letter by letter → *Write ConsentChanged* → tx confirms → Terri's card dims, dashed border, **delisted — consent withdrawn**. The Pulse shows `ConsentChanged`. PIT's review note appears.

> "And now the part that matters most. *(slower)* Terri wants to come home. So — one word.
>
> *(type it, say it)* …**withdraw.**
>
> *(watch the card flip)* That's it. The marketplace delists her, on camera. No email to support, no thirty-day notice. The consent window flips to **withdrawn**, and **PIT** — the very protocol Terri served under at Burning Man — reviews the action, right there in the Pulse.
>
> *(the line — give it air)* Consent is not a settings page. It's a protocol. And the right to leave… is part of launch."
